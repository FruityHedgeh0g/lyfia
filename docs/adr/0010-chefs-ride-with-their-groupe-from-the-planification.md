# A Groupe's Chef rides with it from the Planification, until the Affectation changes

The Chef leading a Groupe is signed up for every Event of its Secteur from the Event's Planification on, as a pilote riding with that Groupe, with a confirmed place. A Groupe does not ride without its Chef, so that place is not like any other: it is kept beyond the Event's and the Groupe's maximums, it asks for no phone number (the Chef was signed up, not signing up), and neither the Chef nor the Bureau can withdraw, remove, or take them out of the Groupe. Only the Affectation moves it: until the Event is under way (En cours, Archivé) or Annulé, a Groupe's new Chef is signed up with it, taking a sign-up they already had (as a passager, on the Liste d'attente, in another Groupe) and making it theirs, while the previous Chef stays signed up, without a Groupe, an ordinary Participant again. The Chef sees their Groupe's Event while it is still in Planification, which otherwise only the Bureau sees, so that they can prepare their Groupe.

## Considered Options

- **The Chef signs up like anyone, once the Event is Ouvert**: rejected; a Groupe could find its own Chef on the Liste d'attente, or missing.
- **Signed up once, at the Event's creation, and left alone afterwards**: rejected; the first change of Chef would leave a Groupe led by someone not riding with it.
- **The Chef may withdraw, or the Bureau remove them, like any Participant**: rejected; the Groupe would ride without its Chef. Handing the Groupe to someone else is the way out, and it moves the place with it.

## Consequences

- An Event's Participants may exceed its maximum by its Chefs (and the passagers who follow them).
- No sign-up records that it was made for a Chef: the place is the Chef's while that person leads the Groupe they ride with, so it follows the Affectation without a flag to keep in step.
- Events created before this rule have no Chefs signed up; there is no production data yet, so none are backfilled.
