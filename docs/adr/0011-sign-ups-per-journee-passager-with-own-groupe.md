# Sign-ups per Journée, and a passager with a Groupe of their own

An Event spanning several days takes sign-ups per Journée (each calendar date between its start and its end): people come for one day or for all of them, and the maximum the Bureau sets caps how many people the Event receives each Journée, for their physical safety, whatever their Poste (conducteur, passager, chauffeur camionnette, point fixe, service). The Poste and the Groupe stay those of the Event, the same every Journée; only the pairing of a passager with a conducteur goes Journée by Journée. A passager is no longer a follower of their conducteur: they have a sign-up and a Groupe of their own, and naming a conducteur when signing up ("je viens avec") only saves time and keeps the pair in one Groupe. A conducteur takes at most one passager per Journée; the pair waits and moves up together, and whoever withdraws a conducteur, removes them or moves them to another Groupe chooses whether the passager goes along or the pairing ends.

## Considered Options

- **One sign-up for the whole Event**: rejected; people come for a single day, and a maximum counted over the whole Event says nothing about how many people are there on a given day.
- **Poste and Groupe chosen per Journée**: rejected; a Groupe whose people change every day cannot be prepared by its Chef, and each Demande de groupe would have to be decided day by day.
- **The passager follows their conducteur entirely** (the previous rule: withdrawn with them, in their Groupe, with any number of passagers): rejected; a club motorbike carries one passager, a passager left without their conducteur on a day should be paired with another one of the Groupe rather than lose their place, and someone coming as a passager may not know yet behind whom.

## Consequences

- A Demande de groupe is accepted only if the Groupe has room every Journée the person is signed up for.
- Shortening an Event removes the sign-ups of the Journées it no longer has.
- A passager may stand without a conducteur on a Journée; the Bureau and the Chef de groupe see it and pair them again.
- The conducteur a passager comes with is named by typing their first and last name, matched on the server among the conducteurs signed up: the list of who is signed up is never served to the people signing up. A name matching nobody, or several people about as well, is refused, and the passager corrects it or signs up without a conducteur.
