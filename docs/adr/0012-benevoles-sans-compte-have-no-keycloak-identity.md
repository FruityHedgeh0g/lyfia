# A Bénévole sans compte is a person without a Keycloak identity

Some people who ride with the association have no phone or no internet and cannot register on the site. The Bureau records them instead, as a Bénévole sans compte, and signs them up for Events under the same rules as anyone (Poste, Journées, maximums, Liste d'attente, the Inscription aux événements Feature). They are people in their own right, in the `users` table like everyone else: they come back from one Event to the next, count towards the maximums, appear among the Inscrits and can be promoted to Membre. Unlike everyone else, their identifier is generated by the site rather than taken from a Keycloak account, and they are marked as having no account: they never sign in, the Keycloak mirror of their Role (ADR 0002) is skipped, and, having no Mon espace, the Bureau keeps their phone number and birth date up to date (their names stay an Admin's, ADR 0007).

## Considered Options

- **A free line on the Event's roster** (a name and a Poste, no person behind it): rejected; it would have to be typed again at each Event, could not be promoted to Membre, and every rule written for a person (pairing, Demandes, maximums, exports) would need a second case.
- **The Bureau creates a real Keycloak account on their behalf**: rejected; it needs an e-mail address these people do not have or will not use, and leaves accounts nobody signs in to.

## Consequences

- The phone number required to sign up is not asked of a Bénévole sans compte.
- A minor's décharge is signed on paper and recorded by the Bureau, which keeps which Bureau member recorded it.
- Should a Bénévole sans compte register on the site later, they get a second person: linking the two is left for later.
