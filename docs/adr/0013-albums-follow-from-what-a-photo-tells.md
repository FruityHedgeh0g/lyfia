# An Album follows from what a photo tells about itself

The Galerie is organised in Albums: the Photos de la Galerie of one Secteur for one Année, browsed by Event (often the Secteur's édition, its big Event of the year) or by Groupe. An Album is never created nor filled by hand: each photo tells which Secteur it belongs to, which Année it is of (its Event's, or given by the Bureau when it has none), and optionally which Event and which Groupes it shows, and the Albums follow. A photo showing two Groupes appears under both.

## Considered Options

- **Albums created and filled by hand by the Bureau**: rejected; a second structure to keep up beside the Events and Groupes the association already has, and a photo filed in one album only.

## Consequences

- There is nothing to set up for a new year or a new Event: its photos make its Album.
- An Album cannot be renamed, given a cover nor ordered by hand; photos follow their Event (latest first), then the order they were deposited in.
